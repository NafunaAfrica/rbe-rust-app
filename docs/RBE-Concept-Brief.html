The platform behind the store

From rented
to owned.

Why we rebuilt the RBE storefront as a custom platform — and what that unlocks for the business next.

01

The short version

RBE started on Lovable — a hosted, low-code builder that generates a React site backed by Supabase. It got the idea online fast, but you were renting a template: limited to what the platform allows, paying monthly for the privilege, and unable to plug in the tools a real print-on-demand store needs. We rebuilt it as a custom platform you own outright — same look, but now the store, the database, and the Shopify + Printify connections are all yours to shape. It is live today at tanya.nafuna.africa.

02

What actually changed

Before

Lovable + React + Supabase — a hosted builder
  • ›You rent the platform — monthly fees that climb as the store grows.
  • ›Boxed in to the builder's features; new ideas wait on what it supports.
  • ›Your customer & order data lives inside someone else's system.
  • ›Integrations are shallow — whatever the template exposes, no more.
  • ›If the vendor changes terms or prices, you go along with it.

Now

Custom build — Rust engine + your own database
  • ✓You own the code and the data — no per-seat or per-feature ceiling.
  • ✓Any feature is on the table; the store bends to the business, not the reverse.
  • ✓Customers, orders and content sit in your database.
  • ✓Shopify (checkout) and Printify (printing) are wired in directly and deeply.
  • ✓Runs as one small, fast server — low, predictable hosting cost.

The template got RBE online. Owning the platform is what lets it grow.

03

Why it moves the business ahead

Ownership.

The whole store — code, data, design — belongs to RBE. No monthly platform tax that grows with success, and nothing that can be switched off or priced up from the outside. What you build, you keep.

Flexibility.

Because it's custom, there is no "the platform doesn't do that." A loyalty scheme, a members' drop, a wholesale portal, a new payment method — each becomes a build, not a dead end. The roadmap is yours to write.

Deeper
integrations.

Shopify handles secure checkout; Printify prints and ships. We talk to both of them directly, so orders, fulfilment and tracking can flow straight back into your own dashboard — the kind of connection a hosted template simply can't reach.

Speed
& cost.

The site is built in Rust and runs as a single lightweight server. Pages render fast for shoppers, and hosting is one small, predictable line item instead of a subscription that scales with your traffic.

Your data.

Customers, orders, and sales history are captured in your own database — so analytics, reporting and marketing are built on numbers you control, not exports begged from a third party.

04

How it fits together

One RBE-owned server sits at the centre. Shoppers only ever see tanya.nafuna.africa; behind it, Shopify takes payment and Printify prints and ships — and every order flows back into your dashboard.

The bottom line

A hosted template is a great way to start. But a growing brand outgrows the box it was started in. RBE now runs on a platform it owns — faster, cheaper to run, and open to any idea the business wants to chase. The store you can see today is the foundation; the roadmap above is what owning it makes possible.
